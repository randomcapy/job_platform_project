⚙️ How Semantic Matching Works

1

PDF Parsing

PyMuPDF (fitz) extracts all text from the PDF resume, page by page, preserving reading order.

2

SBERT Embedding

Sentence-BERT encodes resume & job text into 384-dimensional semantic vectors via a fine-tuned transformer.

3

Cosine Similarity

Measures the cosine of the angle between vectors. Closer direction = higher semantic similarity score.

4

Threshold & Rank

Jobs sorted by score. Below threshold (0.35) → external platform fallback suggestions shown.
